About Our Project

We created Echoes of Wisdom to build meaningful connections between students and the elderly. Through visits, conversations, storytelling, and activities, we hope to give them companionship while learning from their experiences and stories.

Our Activities

💬 Storytelling

Talking with residents and sharing valuable life experiences.

🎲 Engagement

Playing games and bringing joy to their leisure time.

🤝 Assistance

Helping with daily activities and providing physical support.

📸 Memories

Taking photos to preserve beautiful moments together.
